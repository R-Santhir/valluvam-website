Where we work

Sri Lanka — the villages at the heart of our mission

Our programs are concentrated in three remote villages in northern Sri Lanka, where Tamil families continue to rebuild after decades of conflict and displacement. These communities face persistent challenges in housing, healthcare, and education — and they are the reason Valluvam exists.
