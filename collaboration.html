Why partner with Valluvam

Building a broader network of solidarity

Valluvam works at the intersection of grassroots community development and diaspora-led advocacy. We partner with organisations who bring complementary expertise, resources, and reach to amplify our shared mission.

Whether you're an NGO, a community organisation, a healthcare provider, or a diaspora group — if you're committed to the wellbeing of Tamil communities, there's a place for us to work together.

Community organisations

Diaspora groups, Tamil cultural associations, and local community centres across Canada and Southeast Asia.

Healthcare & social services

Clinics, hospitals, and social service providers who can extend care to underserved Tamil communities.

Funders & foundations

Grant-makers and foundations aligned with human rights, gender equity, and community development in the Global South.

International NGOs

Organisations operating in Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia who can support or connect with our on-the-ground programs.
