About the program

Good food that funds a greater purpose

Kumaran Kudil குமரன் குடில் is Valluvam's volunteer-run fundraising initiative based in the Greater Toronto Area. Every week, a dedicated team of volunteers prepares and sells authentic Tamil snacks, meals, and catering — and every dollar of profit goes directly toward funding Valluvam's programs in Sri Lanka and the diaspora.

This project is completely run by volunteers. There are no salaries, no overhead — just community members cooking together with care and purpose. Meal options and prices are posted regularly on Valluvam's Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

To place a catering order or join the weekend meals WhatsApp group, see the details below.

What we do

What Kumaran Kudil offers

Catering services

Vegetarian and non-vegetarian authentic Tamil food catered for events of 25 to 800 people. Our volunteers work tirelessly to ensure every dish is made with care using authentic recipes. To discuss catering orders, call 416 871 5035.

Weekend meals

Authentic Tamil lunches available on Saturdays and Sundays. Join the WhatsApp group for weekly menus and prices: join here.

All profits fund Valluvam's mission

Every dollar earned through Kumaran Kudil goes directly to Valluvam's programs — housing support, refugee assistance, women's health, and community development across Sri Lanka and the diaspora.
