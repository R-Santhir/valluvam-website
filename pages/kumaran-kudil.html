About the program

Every child deserves a foundation to grow

Kumaran Kudil — meaning "children's home" in Tamil — supports children in the villages of Puthur-Kathiraveli, Karungkali Cholai, and Kallaripu-Verukal who face barriers to education and a stable upbringing due to poverty, family hardship, or the lingering impacts of conflict.

Many young people in these communities are forced to skip school simply because they lack basic necessities — school supplies, proper clothing, nutritious meals, or a safe space to study. Kumaran Kudil addresses these gaps directly, helping to break the cycle of poverty by ensuring children can stay in school and reach their full potential.

By supporting children today, we invest in the leaders, educators, and community builders of tomorrow.

What we do

How Kumaran Kudil supports children

School supplies & materials

Providing notebooks, stationery, bags, and other essential school materials so children are equipped and ready to learn.

Nutritional support

Ensuring children have access to regular, nutritious meals — removing hunger as a barrier to attending school and concentrating in class.

Safe shelter & care

Supporting children who lack stable home environments with safe shelter arrangements and the care of trusted community members.

After-school support

Tutoring, homework help, and supervised study time for children who lack a quiet space or adult support at home.

Family support

Working with parents and caregivers to address the root causes preventing children from attending school and accessing opportunities.

Celebrating achievements

Recognising and celebrating children's academic and personal achievements — building confidence and pride in their community.
