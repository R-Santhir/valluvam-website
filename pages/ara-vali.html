About the program

Empowering Tamil women to lead and thrive

Ara-Vali — meaning "strength" or "force" in Tamil — is Valluvam's most comprehensive women's empowerment program. Launched in 2024, it brings together a wide range of activities designed to build skills, wellbeing, community bonds, and economic opportunity for women across all three villages.

Tamil women in these communities carry enormous responsibilities — as caregivers, providers, and community pillars — often with very little institutional support. Ara-Vali recognises their existing strength and creates space for it to grow further through practical skills, peer connection, and cultural celebration.

The program takes a holistic approach, addressing not just economic needs but also physical wellness, mental wellbeing, cultural identity, and social connection — understanding that true empowerment is multi-dimensional.

What we do

Ara-Vali program activities

The program spans a broad range of activities — each chosen based on the expressed needs and interests of women in the communities themselves.

Economic skills & livelihood

Practical training in income-generating skills — sewing, crafts, small business management — giving women greater financial independence.

Peer support & women's circles

Facilitated peer support groups where women share experiences, challenges, and strategies — reducing isolation and building collective resilience.

Physical wellness

Yoga, stretching, and movement sessions designed to support physical health and stress relief for women carrying heavy daily responsibilities.

Cultural programs

Celebrating Tamil heritage through cultural activities, traditional arts, and community gatherings that strengthen identity and pride.

Literacy & education

Supporting women who missed educational opportunities with literacy programs and continuing education classes.

Leadership development

Identifying and supporting emerging women leaders within the community — building the capacity for long-term, self-sustaining community development.
