About the program

Every family deserves a safe place to call home

Kudil Mempadu — meaning "home improvement" in Tamil — is Valluvam's core housing support program. In the three remote villages we serve, many families live in homes that were damaged or destroyed during decades of conflict, or that were never properly built to begin with.

Through Kudil Mempadu, Valluvam provides direct material support — construction supplies, roofing materials, flooring — alongside community-organised labour and skills sharing, so that families can build and repair their homes with dignity and agency.

The program also encompasses a thrift store initiative where donated clothing, furniture, and household items are made available to families in need, and a case management service connecting adults with social services and resources.

What we do

Three pillars of Kudil Mempadu

Home construction & repair

Providing roofing materials, flooring, structural supports, and other construction essentials for families rebuilding or repairing their homes in all three villages.

Thrift store donations

Gently used clothing, furniture, and household items collected from the Canadian Tamil community are made available to families in Sri Lanka and locally to those in need.

Case management support

Connecting adults — particularly Tamil newcomers in Canada — with social services, community resources, and referrals. Operated by Gesland (Rajan) Gnanapragasam MSW, RSW.

Community coordination

Working with local leaders and community members in each village to identify priorities, organise labour, and ensure that support reaches those who need it most.

Financial assistance

Direct financial support for families facing acute housing crises — covering urgent repairs, temporary shelter, or essential household needs during emergencies.

Sustainable building practices

Encouraging and supporting locally adapted, climate-resilient building methods that ensure homes remain safe and liveable long after our direct involvement.
