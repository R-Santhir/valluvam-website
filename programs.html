Each of Valluvam's four programs addresses a distinct and urgent need in Tamil communities — designed with the people we serve, not just for them.

Housing Support

Kudil Mempadu

Helping families in three remote Sri Lankan villages create safe, dignified, and sustainable homes through direct material support and community building. Also encompasses thrift store donations and case management for families in need.

Children's Welfare

Kumaran Kudil

Providing shelter, care, and educational support for children who need a safe and nurturing environment to grow and pursue their dreams.

Refugee & Immigration Support

Ara-Vali

Building strength, self-sufficiency, and solidarity among women in Tamil communities through skills development, peer support, and cultural enrichment.

Women's Health

Malarum Mangaiyar — Blooming Ladies

Providing essential healthcare products and health education to women and children in remote villages — because access to basic health essentials is a fundamental human right, not a privilege.

Our approach

Each program is thoughtfully designed and community-led

We don't arrive with ready-made solutions. Every Valluvam program begins with listening — understanding what communities actually need, what they already have, and how we can genuinely help without undermining local agency and dignity.

Programs are evaluated regularly and adapted based on community feedback. We are accountable to the people we serve, not the other way around.

Community-designed

Built with input from the families and communities we serve.

Directly funded

Donations go directly to programs — no unnecessary overhead.

Dignity-centred

Every interaction respects the agency and dignity of participants.
